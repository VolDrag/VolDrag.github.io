05 /// Research

Published work

Accepted — Pending Publication

Climate Pattern Shift Index (CPSI): A Machine Learning-Based Metric for Analysing Temporal Instability in Annual Temperature Profiles

Hasan Ahamed Alif, Md. Jisan Mashrafi, A H M Golam Hafiz, Iftequr Ahmed, Anupam Sen Sagor, Debashish Chowdhury
Procedia Computer Science — Eighth FTNCT International Conference on Futuristic Trends in Networks and Computing Technologies (FTNCT-08)
Introduces a novel Climate Pattern Shift Index (CPSI) using K-means clustering and Dynamic Time Warping to quantify climate instability in historical temperature data (1980–2023) from Rajshahi and Ishwardi, Bangladesh. Results reveal approximately 30% temporal misalignment between 1980s and 2010s profiles, with Rajshahi showing significantly higher instability (exceeding 1.5 months annually) compared to Ishwardi.
